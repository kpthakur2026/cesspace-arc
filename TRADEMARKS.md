# CesSpace ARC Trademark and Brand Policy

## Purpose

CesSpace ARC is open-source software released under the Apache License 2.0.
The Apache-2.0 license grants rights to use, modify, and redistribute the
software, but it does not grant permission to imply that a modified or
third-party distribution is an official CesSpace ARC release.

This policy describes permitted use of the CesSpace and CesSpace ARC project
names, logos, visual identity, and other project identifiers.

## Project marks

The following are project marks for purposes of this policy:

- CesSpace
- CesSpace ARC
- CesSpace ARC logo and official icons, when published
- Official CesSpace ARC extension names and associated visual identity

This document does not claim that any mark is federally or internationally
registered. Registration status, if any, is separate from this repository
policy.

## Permitted uses

You may use the CesSpace ARC name truthfully to:

- state that software is compatible with CesSpace ARC;
- state that your software is based on or derived from CesSpace ARC;
- link to the official CesSpace ARC repository;
- discuss, review, teach, benchmark, or document CesSpace ARC;
- identify an unmodified official CesSpace ARC release.

Examples:

- "Compatible with CesSpace ARC"
- "Based on CesSpace ARC"
- "Fork of CesSpace ARC"

## Uses requiring a different product identity

If you fork, modify, repackage, or redistribute CesSpace ARC in a way that
creates a distinct product or distribution, you must not present that product
as an official CesSpace ARC release.

A modified distribution should use a clearly different product name and
branding and should make its relationship to the upstream project clear.

Do not use:

- "Official CesSpace ARC"
- the official CesSpace ARC logo as the primary identity of a modified fork;
- naming or presentation that reasonably suggests endorsement, sponsorship,
  certification, or publication by the official CesSpace ARC project when none
  exists.

## Official releases and extensions

Only artifacts, releases, extensions, and packages explicitly identified by
the official CesSpace ARC publisher may be described as official CesSpace ARC
artifacts.

The canonical source of official publisher information is PUBLISHER.md in the
official repository:

https://github.com/kpthakur2026/cesspace-arc

A third-party package, MCP server, third-party extension or integration,
container image, binary, installer, or hosted service must not claim to be an
official CesSpace ARC distribution unless the official publisher has
explicitly published or authorized it.

## Forks and derivatives

The Apache-2.0 license continues to govern source-code rights.

This brand policy does not prohibit forks or derivative works. It only requires
that users can distinguish independent derivatives from official CesSpace ARC
releases.

## No implied endorsement

Use of the source code or compatibility with CesSpace ARC does not imply
endorsement by the CesSpace ARC project, its maintainers, or publisher.

## Questions

For questions about brand usage, open a non-security GitHub discussion or issue
in the official repository unless another official contact channel is
published.
